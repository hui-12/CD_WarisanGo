Title: WarisanGo

A responsive website (desktop + mobile browser only, NOT a native app
Dont add any FR / UC or similar to the UI that helps tourists discover Malaysian 
heritage food businesses (century-old kopitiams, traditional restaurants, 
heritage street food stalls) as part of the Visit Malaysia 2026 (VM2026) 
tourism campaign. It has two user roles: Tourist and Admin.

UI: according to design.txt

--- User Management Module --- 

FR1.1: The system shall allow new tourists to sign up using their Google credentials.
FR1.2: The system shall allow returning tourists to log in using their Google credentials.
FR1.3: The system shall allow a logged-in user to log out, terminating their active session on that device.
FR1.4: The system shall allow tourists to view their personal profile details.
FR1.5: The system shall allow tourists to update their personal profile details.
FR1.6: The system shall display a list of heritage businesses previously visited by the tourist.
FR1.7: The system shall assign role-based permissions that differentiate between tourist and admin access levels.
FR1.8: The system shall restrict access to admin-only views and functions based on the user's assigned role.

--- AI Heritage Discovery Module --- 

FR2.1 The system shall allow an admin to input keywords to search supported video platforms for heritage-related content. FR2.2 The system shall retrieve videos on supported platforms matching the provided keywords. FR2.3 TThe system shall convert the retrieved video into a written transcript using AI API. FR2.4 The system shall extract structured information (e.g., business name, address, category, history) on the generated transcript. FR2.5 The system shall organise extracted information into json structured fields. FR2.6 The system shall automatically generate a new heritage business record of extracted information. FR2.7 The system shall mark auto-generated business records with a "Pending" status until reviewed by an admin. FR2.8 The system shall store the original video source link, and extracted details. FR2.9 The system shall present all "Pending" status AI-generated business records together in a single review interface for admins. FR2.10 The system shall allow admin view the AI-generated business details by review interface. FR2.11 The system shall allow admins to approve AI-generated business records shown in the review queue. FR2.12 The system shall allow admins to reject AI-generated business records shown in the review queue. FR2.13 The system shall allow admins to update AI-generated business information before approval. FR2.14 The system shall record the identity of the admin who approved or rejected a listing. FR2.15 The system shall record the time of each approval or rejection action. FR2.16 The system shall update the AI-generated business records status of each records after admin approved or rejected. FR2.17 The system shall allow admin to view the audit log. FR2.18 The system shall display admin name, data, AI-generated business title, and status. FR2.19 The system shall allow admin view the AI-generated business details by audit log.

--- Heritage Business Directory Module --- 

FR3.1: The system shall display all heritage business listings that have passed admin review. FR3.2: The system shall display full details of a heritage business when a user selects it. FR3.3: The system shall allow users to filter heritage business listings by category. FR3.4: The system shall allow users to filter heritage business listings by state. FR3.5: The system shall allow users to filter heritage business listings by city. FR3.6: The system shall allow users to search for a heritage business by name. FR3.7: The system shall display a photo gallery of images associated with each heritage business.

--- Interactive Map and Navigation Module ---

FR4.1: The system shall display an interactive map. FR4.2: The system shall display all approved heritage businesses as markers on the interactive map. FR4.3: The system shall allow tourists to click a marker to view information like name, location, category, and rating of that heritage business. FR4.4: The system shall redirect tourists to Google Maps navigation after selecting “Go to Google Maps” on the view information section. FR4.5: The system shall detect the tourist's current location to enable location-based features. FR4.6: The system shall display the tourists’ current location on the interactive map. FR4.7: The system shall allow tourists to filter map markers by state FR4.8: The system shall allow tourists to filter map markers by city FR4.9: The system shall allow tourists to filter map markers by search result FR4.10: The system shall allow tourists to filter map markers by nearby location, which is within a user-selected distance from the tourist's current location.

--- Review And Rating Module --- 

FR5.1: The system shall allow tourists to submit a review for a heritage business, with optional star ratings and photo uploads. 
FR5.2: The system shall allow tourists to comment on a submitted review.
FR5.3: The system shall allow tourists to edit to a submitted review.
